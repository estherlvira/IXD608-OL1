01 Ceremonial Grade: The Purist's Choice

This is matcha at its most refined. Harvested from only the youngest, most tender leaves picked in the first flush of spring, then shaded, steamed, and stone-ground to a silky, vivid emerald powder. The flavor is delicate, naturally sweet, and almost entirely free of bitterness — meant to be tasted, not masked.

02 The Everday Ritual: Premium Grade

A step down in ceremony, but not in quality. Premium grade matcha is bold enough to hold its shape when mixed with milk, yet smooth enough to sip on its own. It's the grade built for real life — mornings on the go, afternoon pick-me-ups, and lattes that actually taste like tea.

03 Culinary Grade: The Kitchen Workhorse

Bolder, earthier, and more assertive — culinary grade is made to compete. When matcha needs to stand up to chocolate, vanilla, sugar, or an oven, this is the grade that won't get lost in the mix. It's less about sipping and more about cooking.
